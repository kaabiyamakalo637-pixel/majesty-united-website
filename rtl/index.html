About the League

Rising Talents League provides a structured competition where young players gain experience, demonstrate their abilities and earn opportunities for further development.

01 / DEVELOPMENT

Player Development

Promoting technical ability, tactical understanding, discipline and competitive experience.

02 / COMPETITION

Organised Football

A structured league for participating academies across the Upper River Region.

03 / OPPORTUNITY

Talent Identification

Identifying outstanding players for regional, national and international opportunities.

Two Divisions. One Platform.

Select a division to view its competition structure.

U17 DIVISION

Under 17 Competition

12 academies divided into three groups of four. Each academy plays three group-stage matches. The top two teams in each group and the two best third-placed teams qualify for the quarter-finals, followed by the semi-finals and final.

3Group Matches per academy

The Bigger Picture

Talent Identification Pathway

The league provides a platform to identify outstanding eligible players for Upper River Region representation.

STAGE 01

Competition

Players demonstrate their abilities in league matches.

STAGE 02

Assessment

Performances are reviewed by the authorised technical team.

STAGE 03

Selection

Outstanding eligible players are considered for selection.

STAGE 04

Representation

Selected players represent URR in approved competitions.
