The academy

A clear pathway.
A shared ambition.

Majesty United Football Academy is based in Basse Santa-Su, in The Gambia's Upper River Region. We provide young players with a structured football environment where learning, discipline, teamwork and personal growth matter.

Our approach puts development first. We work to give players regular opportunities to train, improve and express themselves through the game.

Rising Talents
League

Developing Talent. Building Futures.

Rising Talents League is planned as a platform for youth academies in the Upper River Region, with a focus on player development, talent identification and a pathway for selected players to represent the region.

U17U19Planned 2027 launch

RTL 2027 focus

  • Bring academies together through organised competition.
  • Identify promising players across the region.
  • Build a representative pathway for regional teams.
  • Promote fair play, development and opportunity.
