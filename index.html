Who we are

More than football.
A pathway for young people.

Majesty United Football Academy is based in Basse Santa-Su, in The Gambia's Upper River Region. We provide young players with a structured environment to learn, train, compete and grow.

Our approach puts development first. We value technical ability, game understanding, discipline, respect and teamwork. We aim to create opportunities for players while making a positive contribution to our community.

Our missionDevelop players and people through football.
Our valuesRespect, discipline, unity and ambition.

Our philosophy

Development comes first.

We focus on long-term progress and the habits that help players improve on and off the pitch.

01 / TECHNICAL

Master the ball

Build confidence through ball contacts, passing, receiving, dribbling and finishing.

02 / TACTICAL

Understand the game

Develop awareness, decision-making, positioning and teamwork in realistic situations.

03 / CHARACTER

Grow as a person

Promote discipline, respect, responsibility, resilience and a strong team spirit.
